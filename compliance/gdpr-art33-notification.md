# Personal data breach notification under GDPR Art. 33 (simulated)

> This is a training template. The Berlin authority has its own online form, and its fields take priority over this layout.

**To:** Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information
**From:** St. Aurelia Klinikum gGmbH (controller); DPO: [name, email, phone]
**Sent:** Mon [date], within 72 h of awareness (Fri 18:30)

| Art. 33(3) requirement | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| (a) Nature of the breach | Confidentiality breach (exfiltration) and availability breach (encryption) caused by a ransomware attack |
| (a) Categories of data subjects | Patients (current and former) |
| (a) Approximate number of data subjects | about 500 (lab dataset); in the scenario, all patients in the EHR |
| (a) Categories of records | Identification data (name, date of birth, address, phone); health insurance number; **health data (Art. 9):** diagnoses (ICD-10), lab results, department and stay dates |
| (b) DPO contact | [name, email, phone] |
| (c) Likely consequences | Exposure of sensitive health information; extortion of individual patients; identity and insurance fraud; distress; delayed treatment during downtime |
| (d) Measures taken or proposed | Accounts reset, hosts isolated, export function restricted, police involved, leak-site monitoring, patient notification (Art. 34), patient helpline, MFA for all remote access |
| Phased notification (Art. 33(4)) | Yes. Final numbers follow after forensic analysis |
