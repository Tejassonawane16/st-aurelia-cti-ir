# Notice to patients under GDPR Art. 34 (simulated)

> Art. 34(2) requires clear and plain language. In a real case, send it in German and English, and in other languages common among the patients.

**Subject: Important information about your personal data at St. Aurelia Klinikum**

Dear [patient name],

On [date], St. Aurelia Klinikum was hit by a criminal cyberattack. The attackers copied information from our patient record system. We are writing because your information is affected.

**What information was taken.** Your name, date of birth, address, phone number and health insurance number. Information about your treatment with us may also have been taken: diagnoses, lab results and the dates of your stay.

**What this could mean for you.** Criminals may publish the data or contact you directly and demand money.

**What we have done.** We stopped the attack, informed the police and the Berlin data protection authority, and strengthened our systems, for example by requiring two-factor login for all remote access.

**What you can do.**
- Do not pay anyone who contacts you about your data, and do not open their links. Report such contact to the police and to us.
- Be careful with calls or emails that claim to be from the hospital or your health insurer.
- Check your insurer's statements for treatments you did not receive.

**Questions.** Our patient helpline [number] is open Monday to Friday, 08:00–20:00. You can contact our Data Protection Officer at [email]. You also have the right to complain to the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

We sincerely apologise for this incident.

[Name], Medical Director, St. Aurelia Klinikum
