# Role cards (print one per player)

**Incident lead (IT security manager).** Runs the response, owns the timeline and decision log, and decides on technical containment.
*Pressure:* you have three people, and it is Friday night.

**CISO.** Accountable for the response. Sends the NIS2 reports to the BSI and briefs the board.
*Pressure:* the board wants answers before the facts are known.

**Data Protection Officer (DPO).** Assesses the risk to patients. Owns the GDPR Art. 33 notification to the Berlin authority and the Art. 34 notices to patients.
*Pressure:* you must judge "high risk" without knowing exactly which data left.

**Clinical lead (Medical Director on call).** Decides anything that affects patient care: EHR downtime, paper procedures, ambulance diversion.
*Pressure:* every hour offline delays treatment.

**Communications lead.** Handles staff, press and patient communication.
*Pressure:* a journalist and patients on social media are already asking.

**Executive board (CEO).** Decides on the ransom and on external help, such as the DFIR retainer.
*Pressure:* cost, reputation, insurance.
